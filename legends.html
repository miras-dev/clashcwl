Legends Day

Your ranked run, battle by battle. Enter a player tag and this page pulls the account's battle log and rebuilds it: attacks used out of what your league grants, what they earned, every defence taken, and where the trophy count stood at the start. Legend I plays legend days — 8 attacks and 8 defenses, wiped at 05:00 UTC. Every league below it is scored over the week, Monday to Monday at 05:00 UTC, on an attack allowance that rises with the ladder (6 a week in the Skeleton leagues, 24 in Legend III, 30 in Legend II).
