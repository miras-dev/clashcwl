Builder Potions & Hammer Jam

A Builder Potion runs every Builder at 10× for one hour. Each Builder banks up to 9 hours — but only if it has at least 10 hours left to run. A Builder on a short job, or with nothing to do, wastes its share.

Where Ore comes from

Ore income is not published as fixed numbers the way costs are, so treat these as the sources to work rather than a rate to budget against.

  • War attacks — the main source. Scoring at least one star claims the full Ore from that base.
  • Daily Star Bonus — from TH8 once the Blacksmith is built; event multipliers (2×–5×) apply.
  • Trader — 10 free Glowy Ore on each weekly refresh, plus Ore sometimes stocked for gems.
  • Clan Games & events — periodic lump sums.

Note: Hammer Jam does not discount equipment. Its 50% off applies to buildings, walls, traps, heroes and the lab — the Blacksmith is excluded, so Ore costs are the same during the event.
